Support

MattyMath — Multiply

Unable to continue a session, have a question about progress, or want to suggest an improvement? Email shakin.dev@gmail.com. You can write in English or Russian.

If possible, include:

  • your device model and iOS or iPadOS version;
  • the app version and build number;
  • your selected app language;
  • what you were doing before the issue occurred;
  • what you expected and what actually happened.

A screenshot or short recording can help, but is optional. Do not include a child's name, date of birth, school or other personal information. Hide personal details in images before sending them.

Testing with TestFlight

Open TestFlight, select MattyMath / Multiply, and tap Send Beta Feedback. You can also find your installed build number in TestFlight. We ask parents or other adult testers to submit feedback.

If progress is missing

Avoid deleting the app or resetting the chart while we investigate unless necessary: progress and practice history are stored on your device. The developer does not receive them automatically and cannot restore them from a server.

Music, sounds and reminders

Music and sounds have separate controls in the app's Settings. Reminders are optional. If enabled reminders do not arrive, check the app's notification permission in iOS Settings. You can still practise without reminders.
