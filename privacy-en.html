Privacy Policy

MattyMath: Multiply on the Fly

Developer: Aleksandr Shakin.

Privacy contact: shakin.dev@gmail.com.

This policy describes data handling in the iPhone and iPad beta version of the app.

Progress and settings

Your fact progress chart, answer and practice history, Sprint results, selected response-time measurements and settings are stored on your device. They are used to select exercises, schedule revision and display progress within the app. This information is not automatically sent to the developer.

You do not need to create an account to practise. The app does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, school or contact details. Practice, explanations and music work without an internet connection.

App data may be included in your device's system backup, depending on your iOS settings. The developer does not manage these backups and cannot restore your progress from them.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and are scheduled locally on your device. The app requests iOS notification permission when you enable reminders in Settings. Declining does not prevent practice. The developer does not use a server to send these reminders.

You can turn reminders off in the app or change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Analytics and TestFlight

This version has no advertising, developer-operated analytics or separate automated crash-reporting services. TestFlight diagnostics and feedback are described below.

When you use TestFlight, Apple processes installation, usage, crash and feedback information. Some information is available to the developer to improve the app. Whether your name and email are visible depends on how you were invited and how you submit feedback. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Contacting support

When you email shakin.dev@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond and investigate the issue. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail.

Parents and other adults submitting feedback do not need to include a child's name, date of birth, school or other personal information. Do not send passwords or payment details. Check screenshots and recordings for personal information before sending them.

Support website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising trackers, our own website analytics, forms or external fonts. When you visit these pages, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Pages documentation.

Website visits, emails and use of TestFlight are handled by the respective external services separately from local practice in the app.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it using the relevant app controls or delete the app from your device.

Resetting progress in Settings deletes the chart, answer and practice history, Sprint results and local recovery copies of progress. No archive is created during the reset.

Your app language, music, sound and vibration preferences are kept. Reminders are turned off after a reset. If the app says it could not finish resetting progress, deletion may be partial: try again or contact support.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Offloading an app in iOS preserves its data; system backups are managed separately. A request to the developer cannot delete data directly from your device.

Support correspondence and attachments are kept only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted unless the law requires otherwise.

Email shakin.dev@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence and information held by the developer. Requests concerning data independently processed by Apple, GitHub or the email provider are handled under that service's policies.

Policy changes

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its revision date. Where required, we will also notify you of the changes and request your consent.
